## Sizing and fit

90 × 150 cm is the standard-width reference size of the range and the cheapest to produce, because it runs on a common 91 cm large-pad line without a non-standard setup. That also makes it the fastest size to sample and the easiest to re-order.

Reference net weight is 60–80 g. Larger bed-sized pads are covered by the 120 × 180 cm and 90 × 180 cm models. Reference values are confirmed on the approved sample.

## Materials and packing

The pad is a five-layer construction: hot-air non-woven top sheet, a tissue or hydrophilic non-woven acquisition layer, a fluff pulp and SAP core, and a breathable PE cast film backsheet. Adhesive tabs are optional on this model and are specified per order.

Layer weights and SAP loading are set to your absorbency target, so the same reference model can be built as a thin volume line or a heavier absorbent line. Standard packing is 10 pads per polybag or printed carton, with barcodes and multilingual labels produced to your artwork.

## Typical use

Floor and crate use — house training, whelping boxes, boarding kennels and veterinary waiting areas. Buyers often start on this size because it samples fastest and runs on a standard width, then move up to the bed-sized pads once packaging and the testing route are settled.
