## Key features

- The same four-layer absorbent build as our washable mat, cut into 500 × 500 mm modules so a single used square can be lifted out and washed while the rest of the floor stays down
- Sealed on all four sides — a cut absorbent core would fray and shed in the wash, so every module is edged before it is packed
- Modules butt together without adhesive. Nothing is glued to the floor, so nothing damages a rented surface and any module can be swapped
- A silicone-printed base holds each module flat instead of creeping, and stops liquid travelling sideways along the floor under the seams
- Damaged or heavily used areas are replaced piece by piece, which is what makes a modular build cheaper to run than one large mat in a busy household
- Cut on the same roll as the large mats, so a room can mix both — a full-coverage area of modules with a single large mat laid over the main accident spot

## Sizing and fit

The reference module is 500 × 500 mm and covers 0.25 m², so 4 modules make 1 m² and a 1.5 × 2.0 m area takes 12. A room is specified in modules rather than square metres, because the replacement unit is the module.

300 × 300 mm and 600 × 600 mm can be produced for volume programmes. Because every module is edged separately, the cutting and edging labour is a larger share of the cost than it is on one large mat — which is why this build is quoted per module and not per square metre of the same material.

Reference values are confirmed on the approved sample.

## Materials and packing

Each module carries the same four layers as the large mat: a polyester knit or brushed flannel face, a high-density polyester–viscose core quilted against migration, a TPU film barrier with PUL available as an alternative, and a polyester knit base with a silicone grip print. The difference is the edge: every module is sealed on all four sides so the core is enclosed before washing.

Standard packing is 8–10 modules per carton, confirmed on the approved sample. Carton print, barcodes and multilingual labels are produced to your artwork.

## Typical use

Living rooms, play areas, hallways, crate trays and car boots where a floor covering has to absorb rather than merely resist wear, and where the household would rather wash one square than the whole floor. Sold as a floor system with the module as the spare part, so a damaged or heavily used square is a small purchase rather than a new mat.

If the floor only has to survive claws, mud and traffic and no liquid is involved, our modular carpet tile range does that job and costs less — it contains no absorbent core and no film, and we do not present it as though it did.
