## Sizing and fit

400 × 400 mm modules, trimmed to shape on site. Coverage is quoted per vehicle class rather than per square metre, and a set normally includes spare modules for the high-wear area immediately behind the tailgate.

Reference total thickness is 15–20 mm with a pile weight of 650–750 g/m². Because boot shapes are not standardised between vehicles, this model ships as a trimmable set rather than a fixed moulded liner — which is also why a single SKU can serve a much wider range of vehicles.

Reference values are confirmed on the approved sample.

## Materials and packing

The face is a cut-pile construction in solution-dyed nylon 6.6, over the same glue-free backing as the interior modular tile — no PVC, no bitumen and no fibreglass. Solution-dyed fibre holds colour through repeated cleaning, which matters more in a car than in a living room.

Packing is by vehicle class in cartons sized to the set. Carton print, barcodes and multilingual labels are produced to your artwork, and sets are quoted on carton dimensions and volume as well as weight so landed cost can be calculated up front.

## Typical use

Car boots, rear seats and camping — sand, mud, dog hair and water caught on a surface that comes out in one piece and goes back in the same way. Also specified as a boot mat for vans and small commercial vehicles carrying equipment.
