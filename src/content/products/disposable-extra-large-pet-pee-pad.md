## Sizing and fit

Disposable Extra-Large Pet Pee Pad is offered in two reference sizes: 120 × 180 cm and 90 × 180 cm.

Production width limits a disposable pad to roughly 120 cm, so 120 × 180 cm is the widest single-piece pad this line can produce. A 183 × 183 cm pad is not achievable on disposable equipment — for full-mattress coverage the options are 120 × 180 cm, or two 90 × 180 cm pads laid side by side as a set.

Reference net weight is 100–130 g at 120 × 180 cm and 70–90 g at 90 × 180 cm. All figures are reference values for the model and are confirmed on the approved sample before bulk production.

## Materials and packing

The pad is a five-layer construction: hot-air non-woven top sheet, a tissue or hydrophilic non-woven acquisition layer, a fluff pulp and SAP absorbent core, and a breathable PE cast film backsheet, with four adhesive tabs at the corners.

Layer weights and SAP loading are set to your absorbency and rewet targets, so the same reference model can be built lighter for volume retail or heavier for overnight use. The backsheet is PE rather than PVC. Standard packing is a handled bag at 6–10 pads; printed cartons, barcodes and multilingual labels are produced to your own artwork.
