## Sizing and fit

The twin pack contains two 90 × 180 cm pads. Laid side by side they cover 180 × 180 cm, which is the full sleeping area of a standard double bed.

This is how the range reaches full-mattress coverage on disposable equipment: production width caps a disposable pad at roughly 120 cm, so two pads at a standard width cover more ground than a single piece produced on a wider line. Each pad is a reference 70–90 g.

Reference values are confirmed on the approved sample.

## Materials and packing

Each pad is a five-layer construction: hot-air non-woven top sheet, a tissue or hydrophilic non-woven acquisition layer, a fluff pulp and SAP core, and a breathable PE cast film backsheet, with adhesive tabs at all four corners. The backsheet is PE rather than PVC.

Packing is 4–6 pads per bag, which is two to three complete sets. Printed cartons, barcodes and multilingual labels are produced to your artwork, and editable label files are supplied so your importer can localise them.

## Typical use

Built for overnight protection of a full bed — for households where the pet sleeps on the bed, or for an older pet that cannot reliably last the night. Also used by boarding kennels, where a sleeping area is laid out as one unit and changed as one unit.
