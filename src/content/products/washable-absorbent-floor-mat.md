## Key features

- Four layers, each with one defined job: the face wicks, the core holds, the film blocks, the base grips. Failures in this category trace back to one of the four being underspecified
- A TPU film specified to a hydrostatic head target rather than sold on the word "waterproof" — mats that leak are the ones where the film was too light for the wash cycle, not the ones with no film at all
- A knitted face is soft under paw and quiet underfoot, and solid-dyed yarn holds its colour through bleach-based cleaning
- A stitched border holds the core in place, so it does not migrate into one corner after repeated domestic washing
- A silicone-printed base keeps the mat flat and stops it creeping across tile or laminate
- Built up to 120 × 180 cm. A width above roughly 160 cm cannot be produced as one piece — it would need a seam, and we would rather say that before a sample than after one

## Sizing and fit

Reference sizes are 60 × 90 cm, 90 × 150 cm and 120 × 180 cm on the same four-layer build. Custom sizes are cut from the same roll, so the constraint is the machine width rather than the size itself: up to about 160 cm wide, 180 cm long is not a problem, and beyond that we would be joining two pieces.

Mats are specified and priced per piece rather than per square metre, because the unit sold is a mat that is washed and reused, not a covering that is measured into a room.

Reference values are confirmed on the approved sample.

## Materials and packing

The face is a polyester knit or a brushed flannel. The core is a high-density polyester–viscose blend, quilted so it does not migrate when the mat is washed. The barrier is a TPU film, with PUL available as an alternative for buyers who already specify it. The base is a polyester knit carrying a silicone grip print.

Solid colours — warm grey, taupe and charcoal — are the standard build. Mats ship folded, or rolled for the largest size, to your carton format. Carton print, barcodes and multilingual labels are produced to your artwork. Because a large mat is bulky relative to its weight, we quote on carton dimensions and volume as well as on weight, so landed cost can be worked out before the order is placed.

## Typical use

Under a water bowl, at a doorway, inside a crate or laid across a car boot, and on the floor beside a bed for a pet that does not always make it to the door. The mat is the reusable answer to the same problem a disposable pad solves once: it is picked up, washed and put back, so the household stops buying consumables and stops throwing plastic away.

Where the requirement is only to protect a surface from wear rather than from liquid, our modular carpet tile range is the cheaper specification — the two are deliberately separate ranges, because the tiles contain no liquid barrier and we do not sell them as though they did.
