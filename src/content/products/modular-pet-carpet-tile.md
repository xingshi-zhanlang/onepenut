## Key features

- A 400 × 400 mm module — the reference size for this category, and small enough that a single replacement tile is an inexpensive purchase
- Lifts out one at a time, so an accident means washing or swapping one tile instead of replacing a whole covering
- Glue-free backing with no PVC, no bitumen and no fibreglass
- Solution-dyed cut pile holds colour through bleach-based cleaning, and does not snag or pull yarn the way loop pile does under a pet’s claws
- Trimmable on a sealed edge for awkward corners, crate trays and doorways
- Compatible with underfloor heating and light enough for a robot vacuum to pass over

## Sizing and fit

The reference module is 400 × 400 mm, with a reference total thickness of 15–20 mm and a pile weight of 650–750 g/m².

300 × 300 mm and 500 × 500 mm can be produced for volume programmes. A room is specified in modules rather than square metres, because the replacement unit is a tile — which is also why this range is not quoted on a square-metre carpet price.

Reference values are confirmed on the approved sample.

## Materials and packing

The face is a cut-pile construction in solution-dyed nylon 6.6 by default, with PTT bio-based polyester available as an alternative fibre. The backing is glue-free and contains no PVC, no bitumen and no fibreglass.

Standard packing is 20 tiles per carton. Carton print, barcodes and multilingual labels are produced to your artwork. Because the module is bulky relative to its weight, we quote on carton dimensions and volume as well as on weight, so landed cost can be calculated before the order is placed.

## Typical use

Living rooms, pet play areas, hallways and crate floors — anywhere an accident, a chew or a scratch would otherwise mean replacing an entire covering. Sold as a floor system with the module as the spare part, which is what makes the category defensible against ordinary square-metre carpet.
