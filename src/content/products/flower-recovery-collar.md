## Product Overview

A soft fabric flower recovery collar that looks cute and works hard — a
gentle, breathable alternative to hard plastic cones for pets recovering
from surgery, skin issues or allergies.

## Key Features

- **Soft fabric design** — no hard plastic edges, comfortable to wear
- **Gentle on the neck** — lightweight, does not choke or irritate
- **Breathable** — comfortable for extended wear
- **Blocks licking and scratching** — effective post-op protection
- **Machine washable** — easy to keep clean

- Materials: soft cotton / plush fabric
- Sizes: S – XL for cats and dogs
- Custom OEM: colors, patterns, logo, packaging available
