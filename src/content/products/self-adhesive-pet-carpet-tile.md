## Key features

- Repositionable adhesive backing — press down to fix, peel up to clean, press back down. No tools and no separate adhesive
- Trimmable with a knife to any shape, so a run can be fitted around furniture legs, doorways and pipework
- Replace one tile rather than the whole covering, which is the same logic buyers are already used to in retail and office flooring
- Lower pile weight and thinner than the glue-free range, which makes this the entry price point of the carpet programme
- Polyester cut pile in a neutral palette, with custom colours available on volume
- Pairs with the glue-free modular tile so buyers can run a two-tier range — a durable line and an opening-price line

## Sizing and fit

Available as a 300 × 300 mm and a 400 × 400 mm module, with a reference total thickness of 8–12 mm.

The adhesive backing is designed for occasional lifting rather than constant removal. If a tile will be pulled up regularly for cleaning, the glue-free modular tile is the better choice for that application — we will say so rather than sell the cheaper tile into a job it will not hold.

Reference values are confirmed on the approved sample.

## Materials and packing

Polyester cut pile over a repositionable adhesive backing, with no PVC in the backing. Pile weight and total thickness are lower than the glue-free range, so this model is specified for light domestic use rather than heavy traffic.

Standard packing is 20–40 tiles per carton depending on module size. Carton print, barcodes and multilingual labels are produced to your artwork, and cartons are quoted on dimensions and volume as well as weight.

## Typical use

Light domestic areas — around a food and water station, inside a pen or crate surround, a hallway runner, or a temporary pet area in a rented flat where nothing may be fixed to the floor. Sold as the opening price point of a carpet programme, with the glue-free modular tile as the step-up.
