## Product Overview

A plush donut-shaped recovery collar that pets can comfortably eat, sleep
and rest in while protected. A soft alternative to traditional hard cones,
designed for post-surgery recovery and skin protection.

- **No hard edges** — pets can rest their head naturally
- **Lightweight and soft** — easy for pets to accept
- **Effective barrier** — stops licking and biting
- **Adjustable closure** — secure fit for dogs and cats
