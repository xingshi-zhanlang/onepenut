## Product Overview

A buffer car seat belt for pets that keeps dogs and cats safe and secure
during car travel. Compatible with standard seat belt buckles, with a
shock-absorbing buffer to soften sudden stops.

## Key Features

- **Universal fit** — works with standard car seat belts
- **Buffer elastic** — absorbs impact and softens sudden stops
- **Adjustable length** — comfortable freedom without roaming
- **Rotating clip** — prevents leash tangling
- **Easy to use** — attaches in seconds
